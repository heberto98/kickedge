## Frozen protocol, recorded before access to 2025 targets

- Verify Phase 5 metadata against tracked report; verify model SHA-256, all original modeling source hashes,
  contract/dataset hashes, runtime versions, model type/alpha/82 feature names, fitted seasons 2016–2024,
  fitted preprocessing structure and row count. Preserve every Phase 5 file/artifact byte for byte.
- Baseline: arithmetic XPM mean over eligible 2016–2024 only; estimate and persist before holdout access.
- Prepare predictions from 2025 **features only**, with artifact/feature whitelist checks and deterministic,
  order-independent, positive/finite, probability/tail checks. Persist preflight report and exclusive
  one-time reveal marker before a single SQL target projection. Join by full unique kicker-game key.
- Keep fitting disabled throughout evaluation with a fit-blocking context; no calibrator is implemented.
- After reveal, save small local ignored frozen outcomes/predictions for audit recovery, not repeated tuning.
  An existing reveal marker refuses another execution, including after a failure. Document any failure;
  do not delete/reset the marker to claim a second blind run.
- Same NLL/RPS/Brier/MAE/RMSE/deviance definitions and ten equal-width calibration bins as Phase 5.
  Independent synthetic equivalence tests compare both metric implementations.
- Paired IID row bootstrap: 1,000 resamples, seed 42, percentile 95% CIs for GLM NLL/RPS/Brier >=2/3/4
  and GLM-minus-baseline NLL/RPS. State that shared games/teams imply dependence and row CIs may be optimistic.
- Calibration: ECE <=0.05 reasonably calibrated; (0.05,0.10] slight deviation; >0.10 important deviation.
  These are operational descriptive bands, not significance tests. Report bin counts and Wilson intervals,
  global predicted-vs-observed rates. No intercept/slope fit or calibrated probabilities.
- Full-distribution bins 0/1/2/3/4/5+ with exact tail. Fixed props Over/Under 1.5/2.5/3.5 only.
- Mandatory subgroups: home/away; weeks <=8/>8; kicker history <5/>=5 prior games;
  lambda <2/[2,3)/>=3. Optional predeclared groups: short/normal/long rest using existing flags;
  recent offense/defense points last5 split at reference 2016–2024 medians.
  Unknowns retained as a separate group. Minimum 50 rows for any subgroup warning/conclusion.
- Drift: Phase 5 top15 coefficient fields, reference and holdout mean/median/std/p05/p25/p75/p95,
  standardized mean difference; abs SMD>0.5 flagged. All82 missingness changes >0.10, new game_type
  categories, outside-reference-range counts. Season 2025 outside 2016–24 is expected calendar drift.
- Error cases: union of largest three overpredictions, largest three underpredictions, three highest
  failed P(X>=3), three lowest successful P(X>=3), fill to at most16 by NLL. Diagnostic only.

## Final gate (do not change after reveal)

- FAIL for an integrity/leakage/probability failure, any threshold ECE >0.15, or lack of consistent point
  signal: NLL and RPS must both be lower than baseline and at least two of three Brier scores must improve.
- PASS requires the above signal, upper95 paired CI of both NLL and RPS differences <0, all three Brier
  scores no worse, all ECE<=0.05, absolute global threshold-probability biases<=0.05, and no warning below.
- Otherwise PASS WITH CAUTION when point signal exists without a FAIL condition.
- Warnings: >10% NLL or RPS deterioration against published 2024 metrics; subgroup n>=50 with NLL
  >baseline by >20%; missingness shift >10 percentage points in a top15 field; an unseen game_type category.
  Subgroup/year comparisons are descriptive, not claims of statistical significance. Numeric cutoffs are
  conservative operational decisions made before reveal; they do not certify betting profitability.

## Selection rules registered before candidate results

The pooled 2020–2025 OOF results are development evidence, not an untouched test:
the diagnostic audit informs features, so selection optimism remains possible.
No random folds. The only prospective evaluation in this task is the one 2026 reveal.

- Retain the V1-style baseline unless a candidate improves pooled NLL >=1% and
  RPS >=1%, does not worsen average threshold Brier by >.002, improves NLL in at
  least four of six seasons, and does not worsen weeks1–4 NLL/RPS. Boosting or the
  structural challenger require >=2% NLL improvement to justify added complexity.
- Calibration is retained only if, on rolling calibration evaluation 2022–2025,
  NLL and RPS improve >=.5%, average Brier and average threshold ECE do not worsen,
  and NLL improves in at least three of four seasons. Final calibrator fits only
  historical OOF predictions; retain original raw predictions for audit.
- Refit baseline through2025 even if no candidate beats it, with explicit candidate
  status. V1 remains default unless historical selection passes and the single
  forward evaluation has >=150 common rows, no >2% NLL/RPS deterioration and no
  >.005 average Brier deterioration relative to frozen V1. This is a conservative
  adoption gate, not proof of superiority. Small samples leave V1 champion.
- Never choose by market alignment. No weather/market/new PIT inputs.

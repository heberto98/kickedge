About the model

What KickEdge estimates, how it was validated, and what it cannot tell you.

What it predicts

KickEdge estimates the distribution of a kicker's made extra points (XPM) in one upcoming game. From that distribution it computes the probability that your Over/Under pick wins, loses or pushes.

  • Model: Poisson generalized linear model (GLM), regularization alpha = 0.1.
  • Inputs: 82 pregame predictors covering the kicker's season-to-date and recent history, the team offense, the opponent defense, rest and game context.
  • Fitted on the 2016–2024 seasons and then frozen. It is not retrained or recalibrated.
  • Every input is rebuilt from data available before kickoff (cutoff: one hour before kickoff), with the same definitions used in training.

Validation

  • The 2025 season was held out and never used for fitting or model selection.
  • One preregistered blind evaluation on 2025 was run once and passed.
  • On that holdout the model beat its frozen baseline on log loss (NLL), ranked probability score (RPS) and Brier score.
  • This is evidence about probability quality, not proof of future profitability.

Limitations

  • It is not a betting recommendation system. It does not find, rank or size bets.
  • Validation rests on a single blind holdout season.
  • The Poisson assumption may not capture every source of variance, and the high-XPM tail is more uncertain.
  • Early-season histories are sparse. Missing recent windows are handled by the frozen imputer.
  • Current team affiliation is checked against nflverse player data (team and roster status) when available; game-day participation is not verified.
  • Optional providers (market, weather) can fail. When shown, they are context only and are not model predictors. Weather uses a versioned stadium list and an Open-Meteo forecast, available only within 16 days of kickoff.
  • Multiple selections: each selection is analyzed exactly like a single pick. The combined probability is an approximation that multiplies the selections' win probabilities, assuming independence. KickEdge does not model correlation between selections; same-game selections are flagged and the approximation may be materially inaccurate. Integer lines can push, and parlay settlement rules vary by sportsbook.
  • Prices are entered as decimal odds (stake included). The implied probability of a price is 1 / decimal odds; it includes the bookmaker margin unless both sides are supplied for a no-vig comparison.
  • Estimates depend on the freshness of the NFL source data.
  • There is no guarantee of future performance.

KickEdge provides probabilistic analysis, not betting recommendations. Estimates are uncertain and do not guarantee future outcomes.
